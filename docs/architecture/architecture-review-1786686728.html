Top recommendation

Start with Collapse the duplicated auto-regressive inference loop. It is the highest-leverage, lowest-risk deepening: two real engines vary across an already-there seam, so hoisting the shared loop into InferenceBase behind a single _forward() removes ~40 duplicated lines, gives every boundary-swapping and post-processing change one home (locality), and makes the loop testable with a stub forward (interface is the test surface). It is also the natural prerequisite for the other three inference-path candidates (workflow collapse and boundary-swapping decomposition).
